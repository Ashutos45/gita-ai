🫁 Pranayama for Today

Anulom Vilom

Calming

Alternate nostril breathing to calm the nervous system and improve focus.

  • Sit comfortably with spine straight
  • Inhale through left nostril (4 sec)
  • Exhale through right nostril (4 sec)
  • Repeat for 5 minutes
